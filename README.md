# One Punch Man

## About the Series

**One Punch Man** is a Japanese superhero webcomic created by *ONE* which was later adapted into a manga and finally, to an anime.  
It follows the story of **Saitama**, a hero who can defeat any opponent with a single punch.  
Despite his overwhelming strength, he faces an existential crisis, seeking a true challenge in a world full of absurdity.

---


## Why I Like It
I like *One Punch Man* for its overall aesthetic and creative fervor.  
The manga’s art has inspired and elevated the way I draw over the years, and its storytelling has fueled my imagination and standards for story making.  

I especially appreciate how the **caricatures and characters** are handled, they're so simple yet so iconic.  
They effectively serve as sort of "super-paraodies" of your usual superhero tropes, though not just superhero tropes, but anime and japanese tropes in general as well. It does all this while remaining well-written and fun to watch as these "tropes" are given some genuine depth to them as the story goes on. I just find it so endearing.

---

## Similar Series: Mob Psycho 100
Another series I deeply enjoy is **Mob Psycho 100** by the same author, *ONE*.  
While it shares the humor and creativity of *One Punch Man*, what stood out to me most was its **world-building** and **character development**.  

I’ve developed a deep emotional bond with some of the characters, and the set pieces really amaze me to this day. I think the way the story is told elevates what *One Punch Man* is, in a way. As we got more rounded characters that actually feel like people with emotions and feelings rather than portrayals of parody. And they are handled way more seriously this time.
I’m glad the anime adaptation did it justice, especially considering how especially underrated it is compared to *One Punch Man*.

---

## Conclusion
Both series have left a lasting impact on how I view art and storytelling.  
Their balance of parody, emotion, and creativity continues to inspire me as both a viewer and an artist. The whole reason I improved on drawing was because of *One Punch Man's* art, and the reason why I am so interested in digging into writing is because of *Mob Psycho.* They are both really important pieces of fiction to me and I hold them near and dear to my heart.
